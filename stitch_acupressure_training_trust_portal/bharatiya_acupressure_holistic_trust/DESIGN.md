## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a high-trust, scholarly, and clinically rigorous presence for an institutional alternative healthcare academy. Balancing ancestral knowledge with contemporary accreditation standards, the visual language draws heavily from structured modern learning platforms (such as Coursera) while projecting serene authority, therapeutic legitimacy, and pedagogical clarity.

### Personality & Tone
- **Authoritative & Scholarly:** Precise, disciplined, and standardized like an accredited medical or university portal.
- **Calm & Therapeutic:** Soothing clinical tones evoking healing, holistic well-being, and deliberate patience.
- **Accessible & Transparent:** Clear fee structures, verified credential verification flows, and frictionless curriculum navigation for diverse Indian demographic cohorts (from practitioners to mature career-switchers).

### Design Movement
**Structured Institutional Modernism:** Crisp layouts, restrained surface elevations, purposeful 1px perimeter definition, and refined typography. The UI eschews esoteric mysticism in favor of structured certifications, verified badge metadata, tabular academic transparency, and clean clinical white space.

## Colors

The palette balances clean clinical precision with the distinguished warmth of credentialed institutional education.

### Palette Roles
- **Primary (`#0F766E` - Deep Clinical Teal):** Anchors primary actions, institutional signatures, active navigational states, and verified checkmarks. Represents holistic healing merged with clinical rigor.
  - *Interactive Hover:* `#115E59`
  - *Subtle Fill / Active Containers:* `#F0FDFA`
  - *Soft Accent Border:* `#CCFBF1`
- **Secondary (`#B45309` - Accreditation Amber / Warm Gold):** Reserved for institutional trust badges, ISO/government recognition marks, certification distinctions, and prominent callouts.
  - *Badge Tint Fill:* `#FEF3C7`
  - *Badge Accent Border:* `#FDE68A`
- **Neutral & Structural Foundations:**
  - *Base Canvas:* `#FFFFFF` (Crisp White) for content cards and elevated modules.
  - *Canvas Wash:* `#F8FAFC` (Slate Tint) providing clear distinction between viewport background and content containers.
  - *Surface Container:* `#F1F5F9` for table headers, metadata blocks, and disabled states.
  - *Subtle Perimeter Border:* `#E2E8F0` for card boundaries, separators, and crisp academic outlines.
- **Typography Scale:**
  - *Headings & Key Metrics:* `#0F172A` (Slate Charcoal) ensuring strict WCAG AAA contrast.
  - *Body Text:* `#475569` (Slate Body) for effortless sustained reading across curriculum modules.
  - *Muted & Metadata:* `#64748B` (Cool Slate) for timestamps, certificate IDs, and secondary notes.

## Typography

The typography pairs the geometric authority of **Plus Jakarta Sans** for titles with the pragmatic legibility of **Inter** for long-form curriculum descriptions, forms, and accreditation registries.

### Typographic Directives
- **Numbers and Financial Figures:** All monetary representations (e.g., `₹24,500`), student enrollment figures, and Certificate Verification IDs must use CSS `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` to guarantee vertical baseline alignment across syllabus tables and receipts.
- **Accreditation Titles:** Institutional credentials, ISO numbers, and Ministry recognition labels use uppercase tracking (`label-sm` with `letterSpacing: 0.04em`).
- **Hierarchy:** Ensure tight line-height pairings on hero course headings to prevent excessive vertical drift on desktop interfaces.

## Layout & Spacing

A disciplined 12-column grid anchors the platform layout, drawing inspiration from high-density catalog structures used by global academic repositories.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column fluid grid, maximum content container of `1280px`, with `1.5rem` (`24px`) gutters and `2rem` (`32px`) safe margins. Course catalog filters lock to a 3-column sticky sidebar, leaving 9 columns for course cards.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column layout with `1.5rem` gutters. Course listings reflow to a 2-column card orientation; search filters collapse into a slide-over drawer.
- **Mobile (<768px):** 4-column layout with `1rem` (`16px`) gutters and outer margins. All horizontal course cards reflow into vertical cards with stacked metadata badges.

### Spacing Principles
- Spacing inside course and verification modules strictly follows multiples of `4px` and `8px`.
- Related informational items (such as course title and trainer credentials) use `space-xs` (`4px`) or `space-sm` (`8px`).
- Separation between independent modular blocks (e.g., syllabus timeline steps) maintains `space-lg` (`24px`) to `space-xl` (`40px`).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is conveyed through an architectural pairing of flat surface differentiation and micro-diffused ambient drops, avoiding heavy or saturated shadows.

### Elevation Architecture
- **Level 0 (Base Canvas):** `#F8FAFC` flat surface.
- **Level 1 (Standard Card / Table Rows):** `#FFFFFF` surface bounded by a crisp 1px solid `#E2E8F0` border. Shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`.
- **Level 2 (Hovered Course Cards & Dropdowns):** Subtle upward translation (`translateY(-2px)`) accompanied by an expanded atmospheric blur: `0 10px 15px -3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.02)`, maintaining the 1px `#CBD5E1` border for crisp structural delineation.
- **Level 3 (Certificate Verification Drawers & Modals):** High-layer popovers on top of a `#0F172A` backdrop with 40% opacity blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(4px)`). Shadow: `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.

## Shapes

The system implements `roundedness: 2` (base `0.5rem` / `8px`, `rounded-lg` at `1rem` / `16px`), combining contemporary educational software aesthetics with clean clinical symmetry.

### Shape Hierarchy
- **Cards & Modal Windows (`rounded-lg` / `16px`):** Large interactive surfaces, certification display showcases, and syllabus modules use 16px corner radiuses to present a human-centered, welcoming touch.
- **Form Controls & Buttons (`rounded` / `8px`):** Inputs, select dropdowns, and interactive buttons adopt an 8px radius, communicating operational precision and reliability.
- **Metadata Badges & Chips (`rounded-full` / `9999px`):** Duration tags, accreditation shields, and enrollment indicators adopt full pill geometry for immediate optical distinction from square functional fields.

## Components

### 1. Course Cards (Coursera-Inspired)
- **Container:** Pure `#FFFFFF` background, 1px solid `#E2E8F0`, corner radius `16px`, transition `all 200ms ease`.
- **Header Asset:** 16:9 ratio aspect container displaying clinical workshop photography or anatomical pressure-point graphics, framed with an internal 1px baseline border.
- **Metadata Stack:** Inline array of pill badges immediately below image:
  - *Duration:* `#F1F5F9` background with `#475569` text.
  - *Mode:* `Online / Hands-on Practical`.
  - *Language:* Hindi / English bilingual indicators.
- **Content:** Headline-sm in `#0F172A`, 2-line clamped summary in `#475569`.
- **Footer:** Separated by a 1px top divider (`#F1F5F9`). Left: Tabular fee formatted in INR (`₹18,500`) using `tabular-fee` typography. Right: Text link or primary compact action button.

### 2. Trust & Accreditation Badges
- **Government / ISO Seals:** Encased in a pill badge with `#FEF3C7` (amber tint) background, `#B45309` (amber gold) border and text, preceded by a subtle gold checkmark or emblem icon.
- **Verification Pills:** `#F0FDFA` background, `#0F766E` text, and `#CCFBF1` border for autonomous verification of clinical practitioner licenses.

### 3. Credential Verification Widget
- **Input Field Assembly:** Monospace/Tabular entry field styled with 1px `#E2E8F0` border, `8px` radius, `#FFFFFF` surface, expanding with a `2px` focus ring in `#0F766E` with 20% opacity.
- **Validation State:** Displays verified student name, issuance date, roll number, and issuing trustee signature stamp in an elevated Level-1 card upon successful lookup.

### 4. Interactive Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid `#0F766E` background with `#FFFFFF` text. Hover state shifts to `#115E59`. Active state shifts to `#134E4A`. Radius: `8px`. Padding: `10px 20px`.
- **Secondary (Accreditation / Inquiry):** `#FEF3C7` background, `#B45309` text, `#FDE68A` border. Hover shifts to `#FDE68A`.
- **Outline / Ghost:** Transparent background, 1px solid `#0F766E`, text `#0F766E`. Hover applies `#F0FDFA`.

### 5. Curriculum Modules & Accordions
- **Header:** `#F8FAFC` background on collapsed state, turning to `#F0FDFA` when expanded with a left vertical indicator bar of `3px` in `#0F766E`.
- **Syllabus List:** Clean unordered list featuring custom teal anatomical dot markers instead of generic bullets, specifying lecture hours, practical clinical demos, and point-location exercises.
