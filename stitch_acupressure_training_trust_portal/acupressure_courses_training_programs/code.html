verified_user Eligibility & Entry Requirements

Minimum 10th or 12th Pass from any recognized board in India, OR active Holistic/Ayush/Yoga practitioner, physiotherapist, or allied health wellness counselor. No age barrier.

list_alt Full Syllabus Breakdown

3 Modules • 12 Topics
Module 1: Fundamentals of Acupressure Week 1–4

History of Indian & Chinese pressure philosophy, Yin-Yang equilibrium, Five Element (Panchamahabhuta) cycles, and basic pressure gauge dynamics.

Module 2: Meridian Mapping & Point Location Week 5–8

Detailed anatomy of the 14 standard meridians: Lung, Large Intestine, Stomach, Heart, Bladder, Kidney, Liver, and Conception/Governing vessels.

Module 3: Clinical Practice & Patient Diagnosis Week 9–12

Reflex diagnosis, palpation methods, Acu-probes, magnetic roller techniques, and mandatory submission of 10 live clinical case records.
