Rigorous Natural Healing Systems

Integrating Vedantic Marma Shastra with Evidence-Based Neuro-Reflexology

Established under the Indian Trusts Act, Pracheen Aarogya Research Institute serves as a premier regulatory and educational beacon. Historically, acupressure and Marma therapeutic arts suffered from fragmented documentation, regional variances, and unmonitored teaching clinics.

Our council was founded to solve this dilemma: standardizing meridian charts, establishing precise anatomical point tolerances (measured in standard cun units), codifying reflex-arc biomechanics, and introducing non-invasive protocols recognized by alternative healthcare establishments globally.

Standard Operating Procedure

Affiliation & Quality Control Protocol

The Trust enforces a rigorous 4-step pedagogical audit before granting affiliated status to any medical academy or training center in India.

Step 01 fact_check

Physical Facility Inspection

Mandatory verification of clean therapy beds, sterilized clinical tools, anatomical models, and safe practical training stations.

Step 02 badge

Certified Faculty Mandate

Every instructor must possess a minimum of 5 years active clinical experience and formal Trust Master Trainer accreditation.

Step 03 assignment

Central Examination Papers

Standardized question repositories issued directly from the Central Secretariat, guaranteeing unbiased, non-compromised testing.

Step 04 published_with_changes

Periodic Academic Review

Biannual random center inspections and student satisfaction surveys to renew institutional licenses and retain accreditation.
